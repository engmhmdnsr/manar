Showcasing Advanced Column & Wall Molds at GulfBid 2015

Highlighting circular steel column formwork, single-sided retaining wall frames, and modular shoring at the Bahrain International Exhibition Centre.

📍 Bahrain International Exhibition & Convention Centre (BIECC) 📅 May 5–7, 2015 🏷️ Stand 1B-30 👥 12,500+ Northern Gulf Delegates
High-precision Circular Steel Column Formwork with quick-closing vertical wedge pins exhibited at GulfBid Bahrain.

MANAMA, KINGDOM OF BAHRAIN — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. reinforced its dominant position in the Northern Gulf construction market through its comprehensive showcase at GulfBid 2015, hosted at the Bahrain International Exhibition & Convention Centre (BIECC). As major reclamation developments and high-rise commercial projects expanded across Bahrain Bay, Seef District, and Diyar Al Muharraq, MAO demonstrated specialized formwork and shoring technologies engineered for demanding marine and urban foundation conditions.

A major highlight of the MAO exhibit was our precision-engineered Circular Steel Column Formwork System. Fabricated from high-grade structural steel half-shells, the system produces flawless architectural fair-faced concrete columns without tie-rod holes. Featuring heavy-duty alignment flanges and rapid wedge bolt locking, the circular column molds withstand high hydrostatic concrete pressures up to 100 kN/m² with zero grout leakage, allowing full-height column pours in a single operational cycle.

Single-Sided Retaining Wall & Basement Shoring Solutions

Urban excavation in Bahrain presents unique technical challenges where property boundary constraints or sea-reclamation diaphragm walls prevent through-tie installation. At GulfBid 2015, MAO presented its Single-Sided Supporting Frame System, which transfers hydrostatic concrete casting loads into ground anchor plates through diagonal structural trusses up to 8.0 meters high. MAO’s technical team demonstrated how this system integrates seamlessly with Profex and Profemax panels on complex subterranean parking structures.

Strengthening Bahrain Rental Depot Logistics

GulfBid 2015 generated substantial commercial interest from prominent Bahraini contracting firms, leading to formwork rental supply contracts for multi-story residential towers in Juffair and highway grade-separation interchanges. MAO affirmed its dedicated Bahrain yard operations in Sitra, guaranteeing prompt local delivery, scaffolding erector safety training, and on-site engineering supervision.
