Shoring Props

System Overview & Engineering Concept

Manar Al Omran European Steel Telescopic Props are certified heavy-duty shoring props manufactured in accordance with European Standard EN 1065 (Class D and Class E).

Designed for high-load slab formwork support, wall back-propping, and trench shoring, these props feature forged steel adjustment collars, quick-release captive G-pins, anti-hand crush safety clearance (100 mm), and guaranteed minimum load capacities of 20 kN (Class D) and 30 kN (Class E) across all extension lengths.

100% hot-dip galvanized inside and out, they withstand severe marine and industrial jobsite conditions without corrosion.

Certified Class EN 1065 Class D & Class E

Guaranteed Capacity 20 kN (Class D) / 30 kN (Class E)
