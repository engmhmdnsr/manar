Project Overview

Entertainment & Commercial Mega Development

The Al Nahda Entertainment Complex is a high-profile commercial and lifestyle development in Riyadh, created to provide state-of-the-art cinema auditoriums, family entertainment arenas, and premium retail pavilions.

Manar Al Omran partnered with Consolidated Contractors Company (CCC) to engineer, supply, and supervise high-capacity Ringlock modular scaffolding. The project required versatile access configurations across double-height atrium spaces, high-elevation suspended ceilings, and intricate exterior facade geometries.

SCOPE OF WORK & SERVICES PROVIDED

How Manar Al Omran's specialized engineering division supported CCC from design to site handover.

Modular Scaffolding Supply

Delivered over 450 metric tons of hot-dip galvanized Ringlock scaffolding components, heavy ledgers, and non-slip steel working decks.

Formwork & Falsework Engineering

Engineered comprehensive 3D layout drawings and certified static calculations for high-elevation access towers reaching up to 24 meters in atrium zones.

On-Site Erection Supervision

Provided full-time certified rigging technicians to supervise complex cantilever erections, tie-in verification, and pre-pour structural handovers.

Safety Compliance & Quality Audits

Conducted daily HSE toolbox audits, tag-out verification, and certified load testing in strict compliance with ISO 45001 occupational safety standards.

PROJECT OUTCOMES & PERFORMANCE RECORD

The Al Nahda Entertainment Complex was executed with a 100% zero-accident safety record across more than 180,000 site work hours. The rapid wedge-lock rosette mechanism accelerated access erection cycles by 35% compared to traditional tube and coupler systems, enabling Consolidated Contractors Company (CCC) to achieve early completion of critical cinema hall structures and interior fit-outs to the complete satisfaction of the developer.
