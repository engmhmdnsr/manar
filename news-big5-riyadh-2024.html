MAO Showcases Next-Gen Formwork at Big 5 Riyadh 2024

Exhibiting high-capacity modular formwork, hydraulic climbing cores, and certified shoring systems at the Kingdom's premier construction exhibition.

📍 Riyadh Front Exhibition & Convention Center 📅 February 26–29, 2024 🏷️ Stand: 4D-120 (Hall 4) 👥 55,000+ Trade Attendees
Manar Al Omran executive leadership and engineering directors receiving industry recognition at Big 5 Riyadh 2024.

RIYADH, SAUDI ARABIA — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. Ltd. concluded a highly successful showcase at the 2024 edition of Big 5 Construct Saudi, held at the prestigious Riyadh Front Exhibition and Conference Center. As the Kingdom experiences an unprecedented construction boom driven by Saudi Vision 2030 giga-projects, MAO reaffirmed its 50-year legacy as the leading Saudi provider of high-precision formwork, modular access scaffolding, and heavy-duty shoring engineering.

At our flagship 200m² interactive pavilion in Hall 4, MAO presented full-scale live assemblies of the upgraded Profex Modular Wall Formwork System and the high-pressure Profemax 80 kN/m² Heavy-Duty System. In addition, the technical team unveiled our automated Hydraulic Self-Climbing Formwork (SCF) designed for ultra-high-rise elevator cores and tower shear walls without crane dependency, alongside our certified Ringlock rosette scaffolding manufactured in strict compliance with BS EN 12810 and ISO 9001 standards.

Live Engineering Demonstrations & BIM Workflows

Throughout the four-day event, MAO's senior technical engineers hosted interactive demonstrations for over 5,000 visiting contractors, EPC project directors, and structural consultants. Visitors experienced firsthand the rapid single-lever clamp locking mechanism of Profex panels, which reduces wall assembly labor by up to 35% compared to conventional timber methods. MAO also demonstrated its proprietary 3D BIM integration workflows, showing how digital static analysis and cycle scheduling mitigate clash risks on complex infrastructure basements and multi-span bridge viaducts.

✔ Profex Wall System

Integrated steel frame with 18mm Finnish birch plywood, delivering 60 kN/m² permissible concrete pressure and zero-bolt alignment.

✔ Hydraulic Climbing Cores

Self-climbing rail brackets powered by synchronous 100 kN hydraulic cylinders for crane-free high-rise tower core casting.

✔ High-Load Ringlock

Multi-directional 8-hole rosette joints supporting leg loads up to 45 kN for complex commercial and infrastructure falsework.

✔ 3D BIM Engineering

Detailed Revit and Tekla structural shop drawings with automated static calculation logs and crane-cycling optimization.

"Big 5 Riyadh 2024 provided an exceptional platform to connect with our long-standing contracting partners and demonstrate how MAO’s manufactured-in-Saudi systems are directly powering Riyadh's transformation, from Diriyah Square to the King Salman Park corridors."
— Eng. Salem Bin Hindi Al Harbi, Chairman of Manar Al Omran

Strategic Outcomes & Future Expansion

The 2024 exhibition resulted in preliminary supply and rental framework agreements with multiple Tier-1 Saudi and international joint ventures for upcoming commercial, transit, and hospitality developments in Riyadh, Jeddah, and the Eastern Province. MAO also confirmed the further expansion of its central Riyadh logistics hub, ensuring 24-hour dispatch capabilities of high-load falsework towers, steel walers, and safety staircases across the Central Region.
