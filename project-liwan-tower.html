Manar Al Omran partnered with Maramer Construction Company to supply comprehensive facade perimeter access scaffolding and rigid wall formwork systems engineered to withstand high wind loads during multi-storey structural vertical construction.

SCOPE OF WORK & SERVICES PROVIDED

How Manar Al Omran engineered safe vertical access and shear wall casting systems for Liwan Tower.

High-Altitude Facade Access Engineering

Engineered certified structural calculations and tie-in anchoring patterns for perimeter Ringlock scaffolding climbing across 28 storeys.

Galvanized Scaffolding Package Supply

Delivered premium hot-dip galvanized Ringlock scaffolding complete with non-slip steel decks, safety toe-boards, and high-strength containment netting.

Core Wall Formwork Supply

Supplied heavy steel waler beams and tie-rod systems for casting monolithic elevator cores with superior dimensional accuracy.

Safety Re-Certification & Inspections

Conducted progressive structural safety re-certifications and tie-back anchor testing as the building structure ascended.
