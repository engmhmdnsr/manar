AL Haramaan High Speed Railway

High-precision wall, column, and viaduct formwork systems for Saudi Arabia's flagship electrified high-speed rail corridor.

📍 Jeddah / Makkah, Saudi Arabia 🏗️ Contractor: High Speed Rail Consortium ⚙️ System: Profex, Profemax & H20 Beams
Profex & Profemax
Project Overview

Electrified High-Speed Transit Infrastructure

The Haramain High-Speed Railway is the Middle East's premier high-speed rail network connecting the holy cities of Makkah and Madinah via Jeddah and King Abdullah Economic City across 450 kilometers of track.

Manar Al Omran played a vital engineering role, providing Profex and Profemax modular wall systems and H20 timber beam falsework for architectural passenger station concourses, high-load trackside viaducts, and reinforced tunnel portals.

SCOPE OF WORK & SERVICES PROVIDED

How Manar Al Omran delivered certified formwork solutions across passenger stations and viaducts.

High-Wall Formwork Engineering

Engineered certified static calculations and tie layouts for architectural station walls and abutments reaching up to 9.5 meters in height.

Profex & Profemax Panel Supply

Delivered over 1,500 metric tons of Profex and heavy-duty Profemax modular wall panels equipped with self-aligning clamp locks.

H20 Timber Beam Falsework

Supplied heavy H20 timber beam slab support grids and cantilever falsework platforms for station concourse ceiling pours.

Dedicated Quality & Safety Team

Maintained a full-time resident engineering team for inventory tracking, pre-pour structural checklists, and concrete finish inspections.
