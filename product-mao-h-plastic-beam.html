System Overview & Engineering Concept

The MAO H Plastic Beam is an advanced polymer structural beam designed as a direct, high-durability replacement for standard H20 timber formwork beams in slab, wall, and column formwork grids.

Featuring standard 200 mm profile height and 80 mm flange dimensions, it is 100% dimensionally compatible with all existing formwork clamps, four-way prop forkheads, and steel walers.

Manufactured with internal longitudinal reinforcement and impact-proof solid polymer flanges, it eliminates common timber beam failures such as web cracking, end splitting, and moisture rot while retaining high nail-holding power over 150+ pour cycles.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

H20 standard compatibility, zero moisture rotting, and re-nailable polymer flanges.

Direct H20 Interchangeability

Standard 200 x 80 mm profile fits directly into existing four-way prop forkheads, beam clamps, and walers.

100% Waterproof & Rot-Proof

Impervious to water, mold, and humidity, maintaining consistent structural strength without softening or rotting.

Re-Nailable Polymer Flange

Formulated solid polymer flanges accept standard nails and screws repeatedly without splitting or weakening.

Monolithic Jointless Extrusion

One-piece monolithic co-extruded construction eliminates glued web-to-flange joint separation issues.

Constant Weight & Geometry

Will not gain weight from water soaking, ensuring consistent manual handling and crane payload calculations.

Integrated Impact-Proof Ends

Solid end sections absorb heavy impacts without needing secondary protective caps that loosen and get lost.
