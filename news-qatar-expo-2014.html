High-Capacity Shorbrace Towers at Qatar Expo 2014

Demonstrating 100 kN Shorbrace falsework, heavy steel beams, and transit infrastructure solutions at the Qatar National Convention Centre.

📍 Qatar National Convention Centre (QNCC), Doha 📅 May 12–15, 2014 🏷️ Hall 4 • Stand 4C-28 👥 22,000+ Infrastructure Delegates
High-load Shorbrace shoring tower assembly with heavy steel base jacks displayed for major transit contractors.

DOHA, STATE OF QATAR — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. participated with distinction at the Qatar Project & Infrastructure Expo 2014, held at the state-of-the-art Qatar National Convention Centre (QNCC). Amidst Qatar's extensive multi-billion-dollar infrastructure developments encompassing the Doha Metro, Lusail Expressway, and international sports stadia, MAO demonstrated its specialized engineering expertise in supporting ultra-heavy concrete slab and viaduct loads.

The centerpiece of MAO's Doha display was the Shorbrace High-Load Falsework Tower System. Engineered with high-strength structural tubular steel leg standards and triangular bracing geometry, Shorbrace provides an extraordinary carrying capacity of up to 100 kN per leg (400 kN per four-leg tower). The system incorporates heavy-duty screw jacks with 600mm of stepless adjustment, enabling precise leveling on deep subterranean station shafts and elevated highway flyovers.

Deep Underground Excavation & Metro Shoring Engineering

With major underground transit stations and cut-and-cover rail tunnels underway in Doha, MAO engineers demonstrated specialized falsework configurations utilizing Shorbrace towers coupled with primary HEB steel beams and secondary H20 timber girders. The modular system allows rapid assembly on ground level and crane hoisting in full tower clusters, significantly enhancing jobsite safety and reducing working hours inside congested underground caverns.

✔ Shorbrace Falsework

Heavy-duty leg standards certified to 100 kN leg load with interlocking brace connectors and high-torque screw jacks.

✔ Primary Steel Beams

Structural grade HEB and double U-channel primary distribution beams for deep cantilever bridge and transfer slab pours.

✔ Site Staircase Towers

Fully integrated 4-leg public egress and worker access staircases compliant with OSHA and BS EN 12811 safety regulations.

✔ Static Structural Design

Complete stamped engineering calculation packages detailing permissible ground bearing pressure and wind sway bracing.

"Qatar's mega-transit and stadium programs demand the highest level of structural falsework safety. Our Shorbrace system combines enormous load-bearing capacity with modular speed, giving joint venture contractors total confidence."
— Senior Technical Director, Manar Al Omran Qatar Operations

Mobilizing Qatar Branch Rental Fleets

The 2014 expo resulted in major long-term shoring rental contracts with multinational contracting consortia executing Doha Metro Red and Green Line underground stations. MAO affirmed its dedicated Doha inventory depot, providing immediate delivery of certified Shorbrace components, steel walers, and technical site audit engineers across the country.
