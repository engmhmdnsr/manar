System Overview & Engineering Concept

MAO Plastic Planks represent our premium heavy-duty modular scaffold decking system, manufactured using advanced co-extruded reinforced structural thermoplastic polymers.

Engineered with integrated galvanized structural steel internal stiffening ribs and precision-welded captive end hooks, these planks fit seamlessly onto Ringlock, Cuplock, and traditional tube-and-clamp scaffold ledgers.

Delivering high load-bearing ratings up to EN 12811-1 Class 5 (4.5 kN/m²), superior impact resistance against heavy site equipment, and non-stick polymer surfaces, MAO Plastic Planks eliminate rust and allow dried mortar to be washed away effortlessly.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

High load capacity, integrated safety hooks, and heavy-duty composite formulation.

Steel-Core Hybrid Strength

Internal galvanized steel box stringers deliver high bending resistance and minimal deflection under heavy worker traffic.

Captive Wind-Lock Hooks

Heavy-gauge steel end connectors with spring-assisted safety catches securely lock onto tubular scaffold ledgers.

Extreme Impact Toughness

Heavy-gauge impact-modified polymer shell absorbs hammer blows and dropped equipment without fracturing or denting.

Non-Stick Mortar Release

Cement slurry, wet grout, and adhesives cannot bond to the hydrophobic polymer surface, speeding cleaning cycles.

Anti-Slip Bi-Directional Ribs

Continuous bi-directional grooving drains water immediately and maintains worker slip safety in dusty or oily conditions.

100% Recyclable Eco-Composite

Environmentally sustainable design supporting Saudi Green Initiative standards with fully recyclable polymer cores.
