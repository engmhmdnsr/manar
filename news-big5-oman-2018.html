Expanding Operations at Big 5 Oman 2018

Showcasing certified European steel props, Cuplock falsework, and curved wall formwork at the Oman Convention & Exhibition Centre.

📍 Oman Convention & Exhibition Centre (OCEC), Muscat 📅 March 12–14, 2018 🏷️ Hall 1 • Stand 1E-45 👥 14,000+ Regional Trade Visitors
High-load European telescopic steel props and Cuplock scaffolding exhibited at the OCEC Muscat pavilion.

MUSCAT, SULTANATE OF OMAN — Manar Al Omran marked an important strategic milestone with its successful participation at The Big 5 Construct Oman 2018, held at the modern Oman Convention & Exhibition Centre (OCEC) in Muscat. As the Sultanate embarked on major transportation, port infrastructure, and industrial developments in Duqm, Sohar, and Muscat, MAO presented its comprehensive line of certified formwork and shoring rental solutions tailored to Omani contractors.

At the center of MAO's presentation was the official introduction of our expanded Muscat Rental Yard and Logistics Center, offering immediate regional dispatch of over 10,000 tons of scaffolding standards, Cuplock node systems, H20 timber beams, and European Class D/E telescopic steel props. These props, certified to EN 1065 standards, offer guaranteed load capacities up to 30 kN with built-in anti-hand crushing safety clearances and self-cleaning threaded adjustment collars.

Curved Wall & Infrastructure Formwork Innovation

For municipal water storage tanks, circular siloes, and curved bridge abutments across Oman, MAO showcased the Variform Adjustable Curved Wall Formwork System. With infinitely adjustable turnbuckles, Variform allows contractor teams to set curve radii from 3.0 meters to infinity without needing specialized custom timber fabrication. MAO engineers conducted live radius adjustments, highlighting substantial cost savings on wastewater treatment plants and port silo projects.

✔ European Steel Props

EN 1065 Class D/E certified telescopic props with hot-dip galvanized finishes and high-capacity forged adjustment nuts.

✔ Cuplock Node Falsework

Heavy 4-point node cup locking system for slab supporting structures, industrial access, and offshore maintenance scaffolding.

✔ Variform Curved Wall

Continuous spindle-adjusted steel waler system for circular concrete water tanks, sewage facilities, and highway ramps.

✔ Timber H20 Beams

Austrian 3-ply webbed H20 timber beams with polyurethane end-caps for high bending resistance and durability.

"Our expansion in the Sultanate of Oman guarantees local civil and building contractors immediate access to world-class formwork systems with direct engineering design support right here in Muscat."
— Regional Operations Director, Manar Al Omran Oman

Strengthening Omani Contracting Alliances

During the three-day trade event, MAO held bilateral technical meetings with structural engineers from the Omani Ministry of Transport & Communications, Special Economic Zone authorities, and prominent Omani general contractors. The team secured multiple long-term rental agreements for commercial developments in Muscat and infrastructure packages in Sohar Industrial Port.
