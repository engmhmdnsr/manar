System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Manar Single-Sided Supporting Frame (Universal A-Frame) is a heavy-duty structural triangular truss designed to absorb massive horizontal fresh concrete pressures when wall formwork cannot be tied through with standard tie rods.

Commonly deployed against soldier pile walls, sheet piles, existing foundation bedrock, and deep basement secant pile walls, MAO Single-Sided Frames transfer lateral loads into ground-cast anchor shoes at wall heights up to 9.0 meters.

Equipped with base leveling jacks, heavy-duty rolling gear, and modular stacking sections, the system enables rapid cycle times on heavy substructure pours.

Max Wall Height Up to 9.0 m Single-Sided
Max Concrete Pressure 60 – 80 kN/m²
Frame Material S355 Structural Steel
Standard Compliance EN 12812 / DIN 18218
