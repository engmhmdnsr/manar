Component Systems

System Overview & Engineering Concept

Formwork timber and steel beams are the indispensable load-bearing backbone for modern vertical and horizontal concrete construction across building and civil engineering sectors.

Manar Al Omran manufactures and supplies high-grade EN 13377 H20 solid-web timber beams and structural steel walers designed to distribute concrete pressures and slab dead loads evenly to supporting props and ties.

Featuring high bending stiffness, low weight, and impact-resistant end protection, these beams optimize jobsite productivity and deliver multi-year reusability.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

High Load-to-Weight Ratio

At only 4.8 kg/m, H20 timber beams are easy to handle while carrying heavy concrete loads.

Kiln-Dried Nordic Timber

Chords crafted from selected softwood, kiln-dried to 12% moisture to prevent warping.

3-Ply Solid Wood Web

High-density waterproof bonded 3-ply solid web resists shear stresses and puncture.

Impact-Absorbing End Caps

Molded plastic end caps absorb drops and knocks, significantly extending service life.

Universal Compatibility

Works seamlessly with all slab table systems, early striking heads, and wall clamps.

Full Length Selection

Available in standard metric lengths from 1.90 m to 5.90 m and custom lengths up to 12.0 m.
