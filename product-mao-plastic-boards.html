System Overview & Engineering Concept

MAO Plastic Boards are versatile, heavy-duty composite polymer boards engineered for a broad spectrum of demanding civil engineering and jobsite protection applications.

Available in solid high-density polyethylene (HDPE), ultra-high-molecular-weight (UHMW) formulations, and rigid cellular PVC and PP composite matrices, these boards provide high impact absorption and chemical resistance.

Widely utilized as formwork facing liners, heavy tracked crane outrigger pads, ground protection roadway mats, trench edge protection, and reusable site hoardings, MAO Plastic Boards resist aggressive soil salts and GCC desert sun without rotting or splitting.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Multi-purpose utility, heavy load distribution, and chemical/weather immunity.

Solid & Cellular Options

Available in solid heavy-gauge sheets for maximum impact resistance or cellular profiles for lightweight site handling.

Aggressive Chemical Immunity

Impervious to battery acids, curing compounds, hydraulic oils, diesel fuel, and subterranean sulfate salts.

Machinery Load Distribution

Heavy-duty protection mats distribute crawler crane and heavy vehicle loads safely over soft desert ground.

High Abrasion Resistance

Tough polymer surface resists aggregate gouging, shovel scraping, and concrete poker vibrator impacts.

Zero Rot & Termite Immunity

Unlike timber boards, plastic boards never degrade from fungal moisture decay, ground contact, or subterranean termites.

Modular Step-Joint Edges

Available with tongue-and-groove or overlapping stepped edges to prevent grout leakage and maintain level alignments.
