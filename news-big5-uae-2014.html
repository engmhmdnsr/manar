Live Rosette & Slab Form Demos at Big 5 Dubai 2014

Demonstrating Ringlock multi-directional modular scaffolding and early striking slab drop-head systems at the Dubai World Trade Centre.

📍 Dubai World Trade Centre (DWTC), UAE 📅 November 17–20, 2014 🏷️ Concourse Stand C-142 👥 68,000+ Trade Professionals
Engineered Ringlock multi-directional rosette joints and slab falsework towers demonstrated live at the MAO exhibition pavilion.

DUBAI, UNITED ARAB EMIRATES — Manar Al Omran presented an extensive line of modular scaffolding systems and rapid-cycling slab formwork at the 2014 edition of The Big 5 International Building & Construction Show, situated at the Dubai World Trade Centre. As regional markets witnessed accelerated high-rise commercial construction and hospitality expansions, MAO demonstrated its proven engineering capability to enhance contractor speed while strictly upholding European EN safety benchmarks.

The centerpiece of MAO’s 2014 pavilion was the live interactive assembly of our Ringlock Modular Scaffolding System. Featuring an 8-hole rosette welded at 500mm standard intervals, Ringlock permits up to 8 connections at a single node without bolts or loose fittings. Visitors observed how a two-man crew could erect an entire 6-meter shoring bay in less than 15 minutes with only a hammer, guaranteeing unmatched productivity on fast-track residential towers and industrial plants.

Early Striking Drop-Head Technology

In addition to exterior access scaffolding, MAO unveiled its advanced Early Striking Drop-Head System. Engineered specifically for high-rise cast-in-place concrete slabs, the drop-head mechanism allows primary and secondary formwork beams to be lowered and recovered just 3 to 4 days after concrete pouring, while the vertical props remain undisturbed to support the curing slab. This breakthrough reduces total formwork inventory requirements on multi-story buildings by up to 40%, significantly cutting contractor capital and rental costs.

✔ Ringlock Multi-Directional

Hot-dip galvanized high-tensile steel standards with 8-hole forged rosettes for flexible angular geometric configurations.

✔ Early Striking Drop-Head

Quick-release wedge mechanism allowing early retrieval of beams and plywood while maintaining prop load support.

✔ European Telescopic Props

EN 1065 certified Class D and E steel props with forged collar nuts and anti-crush safety stop pins.

✔ Drop-Forged Couplers

Heavy-duty right angle and swivel fittings tested to 20 kN slip resistance for extreme structural stability.

"Contractors across the GCC demand systems that deliver rapid cycle times without compromising structural integrity. Our early striking drop-head and Ringlock systems exhibited at Big 5 Dubai 2014 provide the perfect balance of European engineering and local Saudi manufacturing reliability."
— Scaffolding Division Manager, Manar Al Omran

Contractor Engagement & Technical Accreditations

Over the course of the trade fair, MAO's technical sales team hosted structured consultation sessions for more than 4,500 visiting structural engineers and procurement managers. MAO demonstrated its compliance with BS EN 12810, BS EN 12811, and Saudi building code requirements, securing new scaffolding rental and sales contracts for commercial high-rises and transit infrastructure across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and Riyadh.
