System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Manar Al Omran Hydraulic Self-Climbing Formwork System is an advanced crane-independent climbing bracket and multi-level trailing platform solution engineered for high-rise towers, elevator cores, and bridge pylons.

Driven by synchronized high-pressure hydraulic cylinders, the entire formwork assembly, working decks, concrete placing booms, and weather enclosures climb safely in a single automatic stroke, even in wind speeds up to 72 km/h.

By eliminating tower crane dependency, the system accelerates typical high-rise core construction cycles down to 3–4 days per floor while ensuring maximum worker safety.

Cylinder Lifting Capacity 50 – 100 kN per Bracket
Typical Floor Cycle 3 to 4 Days
Permissible Wind Speed 72 km/h (20 m/s)
