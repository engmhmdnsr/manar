Project Overview

Institutional Educational Administration Complex

The Ministry of Education Complex in Dammam is a flagship administrative facility developed to oversee regional educational operations across the Eastern Province of Saudi Arabia.

To meet rigorous architectural finishes and stringent municipal deadlines, Manar Al Omran engineered and supplied heavy-duty Profemax universal column systems and modular wall formwork. The system delivered high-speed continuous vertical concrete pours with remarkable structural precision and fair-faced concrete finish.

SCOPE OF WORK & SERVICES PROVIDED

How Manar Al Omran ensured seamless structural execution for the Ministry of Education.

Universal Column Panel Delivery

Delivered versatile Profemax column panels capable of forming cross-sections up to 100 x 100 cm in 5 cm modular increments with fast windmill locking.

High Fresh Concrete Pressure Capacity

Engineered to resist fresh concrete hydrostatic pressure up to 80 kN/m², permitting rapid full-height single pour cycle times.

3D BIM Planning & Shop Drawings

Created coordinated formwork shop drawings, tie-rod layouts, and pouring rate schedules to optimize crane logistics and cycle turns.

Certified On-Site QA/QC Supervision

Continuous on-site inspection and safety certification by MAO field engineers ensuring 100% compliance with Saudi Building Code standards.
