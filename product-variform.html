Variform Curved Wall System

Steplessly adjustable radius formwork for curved architectural walls, sewage treatment tanks, and silos.

Adjustable Radius
Curved Formwork

System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Variform Curved System provides steplessly adjustable circular wall forming from a minimum radius of 2.50 meters to infinite curvature without requiring panel modification or custom formwork facings.

Equipped with integrated heavy-duty mechanical turnbuckle spindles and flexible timber/steel walers, Variform produces perfectly true circular concrete contours completely free of polygon facet lines.

Ideal for municipal wastewater treatment facilities, grain silos, circular vehicle ramps, and signature curved architectural landmarks across Saudi Arabia and the GCC.

Min Curvature Radius 2.50 m (Rmin)
Max Concrete Pressure 60 kN/m²
Radius Adjustment Stepless Mechanical Spindles
Standard Compliance EN 12812 Class 2

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

Stepless Radius Adjustment

Integrated heavy-duty turnbuckles allow exact millimeter radius calibration on site.

Smooth Cylindrical Surface

Pre-Curved Factory Staging

Panels can be pre-curved at MAO facilities to eliminate field setup labor hours.

Modular Height Stacking

Standard panels stack securely to form curved wall heights exceeding 8.0 meters.

High Water-Tightness

Engineered joint profiles prevent slurry bleed on circular liquid storage tanks.
