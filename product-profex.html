Formwork Engineering

System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Profex Wall Formwork System is Manar Al Omran's flagship modular vertical forming solution, engineered to withstand fresh concrete lateral pressures up to 80 kN/m² without deflection or surface deformation.

Built with high-tensile 120 mm steel hollow section perimeter profiles and factory-bonded 21 mm Finnish birch film-faced plywood, Profex is optimized for fast crane-handled gang assembly and rapid cycle times.

With universal hole patterns for easy wall, corner, and column configurations, Profex delivers architectural fair-faced concrete finishes across high-rise cores, shear walls, retaining walls, and civil infrastructure.

Max Concrete Pressure 80 kN/m²
Frame Profile 120 mm Steel Hollow Section
Facing Material 21 mm Birch Plywood
