Project Overview

Mega Commercial, Retail & Hospitality Hub

The Avenues Mall Riyadh is one of the largest mixed-use retail, hospitality, and entertainment developments in the Middle East, spanning massive floor plates, high-ceiling gallerias, and luxury hotel towers.

Manar Al Omran collaborated with lead contractor Nesma & Partners to deliver extensive modular shoring, flying table forms, and multi-directional scaffolding across expansive retail promenades and double-height atrium voids requiring stringent structural load verification.
