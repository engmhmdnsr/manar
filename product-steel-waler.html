Steel Walers & Primary Beams

Heavy structural steel distribution beams for tie load transfer, corner alignment, and waler bracing.

Structural Components

System Overview & Engineering Concept

Manar Al Omran Steel Walers (Twin UPN Channel Walers and Primary Girder Beams) are heavy-duty structural steel elements engineered to distribute tie-rod tensile loads and provide rigid back-propping across large wall forms and single-sided shoring configurations.

Fabricated with continuous longitudinal tie slots and high bending moment resistance, these walers eliminate localized plywood deflection and ensure straight, true wall alignment under high concrete pressures.

All walers are hot-dip galvanized and feature precision splice connections for continuous structural spans across deep pours and bridge abutments.

Profile Configuration Twin UPN 100/120/140
Steel Grade S355 Structural Steel
