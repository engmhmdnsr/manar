System Overview & Engineering Concept

Manar Al Omran supplies an extensive range of EN 74 certified drop-forged steel scaffolding couplers and fittings designed for secure tube-and-clamp scaffold structures, structural bracing, facade ties, and system scaffolding tie-ins.

Manufactured from drop-forged high-grade carbon steel with high-tensile T-bolts (Grade 8.8) and serrated flange nuts, our couplers deliver maximum slip resistance, torsional grip, and corrosion protection across demanding industrial and civil sites.

All fittings are hot-dip galvanized or zinc electroplated to resist aggressive marine atmosphere and chemical vapors in petrochemical facilities.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

Drop-Forged Solid Body

Hot drop-forged construction eliminates internal voids and provides superior fatigue life.

High Slip Resistance

Tested and certified to BS EN 74-1 Class B, delivering slip resistance exceeding 15 kN.

Grade 8.8 High-Tensile Bolts

Boron alloy heat-treated T-bolts resist high tightening torques (up to 54 Nm).

Heavy Galvanized Finish

Electro-galvanized or hot-dip galvanized finish protects against refinery fumes.

Universal 48.3 mm Fit

Connects standard 48.3 mm scaffold tubes at right angles, swivels, or coaxially.

Captive Safety Components

Heavy rivet pins and captured nuts prevent loose parts from dropping at height.
