System Overview & Engineering Concept

Manar Al Omran supplies prime-grade galvanized steel scaffolding tubes manufactured in full compliance with BS 1139 Part 1.1 and EN 39 standards.

Featuring an outside diameter of 48.3 mm and wall thicknesses of 3.2 mm and 4.0 mm, our tubes are formed from high-yield structural steel, continuous high-frequency induction welded, and hot-dip galvanized inside and out to provide extreme structural strength and superior corrosion resistance.

Individually embossed with traceability markings, MAO scaffold tubes are approved for use across Saudi Aramco, SABIC, and GCC infrastructure projects.
