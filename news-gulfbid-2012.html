Expanding Operations at Bahrain GulfBid 2012

Cementing MAO's permanent operational footprint, dedicated rental yard, and high-load shoring solutions in the Kingdom of Bahrain.

📍 Bahrain International Exhibition Centre, Manama 📅 April 24–26, 2012 🏷️ Stand: 1B-22 👥 11,000+ Construction Industry Leaders
High-load shoring towers, galvanized tubes, and drop-forged couplers displayed at MAO's GulfBid 2012 stand.

MANAMA, KINGDOM OF BAHRAIN — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. achieved a landmark corporate milestone with its prominent participation at GulfBid 2012, held at the Bahrain International Exhibition Centre. The expo served as the formal unveiling of MAO’s dedicated permanent branch and extensive equipment storage depot in Bahrain, establishing a direct supply chain between our Saudi manufacturing facilities and Bahrain’s booming infrastructure sector.

At the 2012 exhibition, MAO showcased an extensive array of Heavy-Duty Tubular Shoring Systems, high-tensile 48.3mm galvanized steel scaffolding tubes, and certified drop-forged EN 74 couplers. Tailored specifically to the demanding conditions of industrial petrochemical plants, aluminum refineries, and multi-story commercial towers in Manama, MAO highlighted the exceptional durability of its hot-dip galvanized finishes against aggressive marine and saline atmospheric corrosion.

Site Safety Workshops & Wind Load Engineering

Recognizing the rigorous safety expectations of major industrial and public infrastructure clients in Bahrain, MAO’s senior structural engineers hosted daily technical workshops at our stand. The sessions focused on scaffolding tie spacing, safe working load calculations for deep foundation excavations, and proper diagonal bracing configurations to resist high Gulf coastal wind pressures conforming to BS 5975 standards.

✔ Galvanized Scaffold Tubes

BS 1139 / EN 39 compliant 48.3mm steel tubes with 3.2mm and 4.0mm wall thicknesses and high tensile yield strength.

✔ Drop-Forged Couplers

EN 74 Class A & B right-angle and swivel couplers offering high slip resistance and long service life in marine environments.

✔ Shorbrace Falsework

Heavy-duty triangular frame towers for bridge pier bents, industrial heavy pipe racks, and transfer slabs.

✔ Manar Frame System

Rapid-erect walk-through scaffolding frames designed for safe façade maintenance, masonry, and plastering access.

"Establishing our permanent footprint in Bahrain at GulfBid 2012 allowed us to bring MAO's five decades of manufacturing leadership and extensive rental fleet directly to Bahraini contractors with zero cross-border delay."
— Executive Board, Manar Al Omran Group

Long-Term Supply Partnerships Established

The 2012 exhibition concluded with major supply and rental agreements for key civic and commercial developments across Manama, Riffa, and Muharraq. MAO established ongoing maintenance and recertification programs for contractor-owned equipment, reinforcing our status as a trusted, full-service engineering partner throughout Bahrain.
