System Overview & Engineering Concept

MAO Plastic Panels are next-generation composite polymer formwork panels engineered to replace traditional timber plywood (film-faced shuttering) in concrete wall, column, slab, and beam casting.

Produced from modified polypropylene (PP) with nano-silica reinforcement via high-pressure extrusion into a cellular honeycomb matrix, these panels are 100% waterproof, impervious to moisture absorption, and will not swell, delaminate, or warp.

Capable of achieving 60 to 100+ reuses without surface degradation, they deliver mirror-smooth architectural concrete finishes without requiring chemical release agents (form oil), significantly reducing shuttering costs per pour.
