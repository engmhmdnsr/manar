System Overview & Engineering Concept

Shorbrace Shoring Towers are Manar Al Omran's industry-leading heavy-duty falsework system engineered to support thick concrete transfer slabs, heavy bridge girders, and massive civil spans.

Delivering a certified capacity of up to 100 kN per leg (400 kN per 4-leg tower), Shorbrace combines high-strength tubular steel frames with scissor cross-braces, heavy screw jacks, and cantilever outriggers to guarantee absolute stability at heights exceeding 20 meters.

With only four primary modular components, Shorbrace can be erected quickly by hand or assembled horizontally and crane-lifted into position in minutes.
