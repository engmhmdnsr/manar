Global Presence at Bauma Munich 2013

Demonstrating Saudi manufacturing excellence and European EN certified shoring systems at the world's leading construction machinery trade fair.

📍 Messe München, Munich, Germany 📅 April 15–21, 2013 🏷️ Hall B3 • Stand B3-204 👥 530,000+ Global Attendees
High-load modular shoring towers and precision forged steel couplers exhibited at Messe München Hall B3.

MUNICH, GERMANY — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. proudly represented Saudi industrial manufacturing on the global stage at Bauma 2013, held at the sprawling Messe München exhibition grounds. Recognized internationally as the world’s largest and most prestigious trade fair for construction machinery, building material machines, and formwork engineering, Bauma drew over 530,000 visitors from more than 200 nations across seven intensive exhibition days.

Exhibiting in Hall B3, MAO showcased its full spectrum of certified Modular Scaffolding Systems, Shorbrace heavy falsework towers, and precision drop-forged couplers. The exhibition allowed European and international EPC contractors to witness firsthand the precision manufacturing tolerances achieved at MAO's modern robotic welding and hot-dip galvanizing plants in Saudi Arabia, which operate under strict ISO 9001 and CE conformity accreditations.

Strategic European Technology Partnerships

Bauma 2013 served as a catalyst for high-level technical exchanges between MAO's engineering directors and leading German and European structural testing institutes. MAO validated the finite-element analysis (FEA) testing procedures of its slab table forms and high-tensile steel walers against European DIN EN 12812 and DIN EN 1065 structural codes, opening direct supply channels for European contractors executing complex civil contracts across the Middle East.

"Presenting MAO at Bauma Munich demonstrated that Saudi-manufactured formwork and scaffolding systems meet and exceed the most stringent European structural safety and quality benchmarks."

Expanding Global Trade Relations

Our presence at Bauma Munich significantly expanded MAO's international network of equipment distributors, steel raw material suppliers, and specialized formwork component partners across Germany, Austria, and Italy. The technical agreements signed during the expo continue to empower MAO with cutting-edge manufacturing automation and world-standard engineering rigor.
