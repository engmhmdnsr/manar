Single-Sided Retaining Systems at Qatar Expo 2015

Highlighting single-sided concrete backing frames, bridge table systems, and integrated 3D BIM engineering at QNCC Doha.

📍 Qatar National Convention Centre (QNCC), Doha 📅 May 4–7, 2015 🏷️ Hall 3 • Stand E-40 👥 25,000+ Regional Trade Visitors
Heavy-duty Single-Sided Supporting Frame engineered for zero-tie subterranean retaining walls displayed at QNCC.

DOHA, STATE OF QATAR — Manar Al Omran Trading & Industrial Co. recorded another successful trade fair campaign at the Qatar Project & Infrastructure Expo 2015, hosted at the Qatar National Convention Centre (QNCC). Building upon the momentum of our 2014 participation, MAO unveiled an advanced lineup of zero-tie subterranean wall formwork systems and high-speed bridge decking frames tailored for expressway flyovers, deep metro underground boxes, and sea-level waterfront basements in Lusail and Doha.

The focal point of MAO's 2015 showcase was our Single-Sided Supporting Frame System. Designed specifically for deep excavation retaining walls where property boundaries, contiguous secant piles, or sheet-pile shoring eliminate the possibility of installing opposing tie rods, MAO's heavy modular triangular frames transfer all lateral fresh concrete pressures (up to 80 kN/m²) safely into cast-in-place floor slab anchors. The system accommodates wall heights from 3.0 meters up to 8.5 meters in single continuous concrete lifts.

Bridge Table Innovations & BIM Planning Integration

In response to the accelerated delivery schedules for Doha’s Orbital Highway and Lusail Light Rail bridges, MAO demonstrated its prefabricated Bridge Table Systems. Integrated with hydraulic lowering cylinders and motorized shifting skates, the tables allow contractors to strip, lower, and advance 150m² deck panels in a single synchronized operation. MAO engineers also presented 3D BIM structural clash simulation models, illustrating how digital cycle planning prevents rebar congestion conflicts before equipment reaches the jobsite.

Strengthening GCC Supply Commitments

During the exhibition, MAO finalized major equipment rental contracts for key flyover packages along the G-Ring Road and highway interchanges in Al Rayyan. MAO reaffirmed its commitment to maintaining large local inventory depots across Qatar and the GCC, providing contractors with rapid same-week mobilization and certified field technician support.
