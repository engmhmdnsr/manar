Heavy Civil Formwork

System Overview & Engineering Concept

Profemax Heavy Duty is an ultra-rigid steel-framed wall formwork system engineered specifically for deep foundation casting, hydroelectric structures, and mega civil infrastructure where extreme fresh concrete pressures reach 100 kN/m².

Constructed with reinforced 140 mm high-yield structural steel box sections and precision corner gussets, Profemax accommodates rapid single-lift pour heights up to 6.0 meters with minimum tie-rod penetrations.

The system is compatible with self-compacting concrete (SCC) and rapid full-liquid-head pours, delivering superior dimensional accuracy and low maintenance costs on heavy construction sites.

Max Concrete Pressure 100 kN/m²
Frame Profile 140 mm Heavy Box Section
Max Single Pour Lift 6.0 m Continuous
Standard Compliance EN 12812 / DIN 18218
