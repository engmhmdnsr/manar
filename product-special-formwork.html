Bespoke Engineering

System Overview & Engineering Concept

When architectural visions exceed standard modular formwork systems, Manar Al Omran's Custom & Special Formwork division engineers bespoke steel, timber, and GRP composite molds tailored to exact 3D BIM models.

Fabricated in MAO's advanced manufacturing plant in Dammam, our custom molds deliver impeccable geometric accuracy for complex architectural facades, paraboloid shells, culverts, bridge pier capitals, and decorative precast elements.

Every special mold set is trial-assembled and 3D laser-scanned prior to site delivery, ensuring seamless installation and flawless fair-faced concrete results on signature projects.
