System Overview & Engineering Concept

Manar Al Omran Steel Planks (Metal Decks) and Aluminum Access Stairs deliver the highest level of pedestrian worker safety, slip resistance, and load capacity across all scaffolding and access structures.

Manufactured from roll-formed structural steel with upward-dimpled traction perforations and drainage holes, our steel boards eliminate fire hazards, rot, and moisture degradation associated with traditional timber boards.

Equipped with integrated wind-lock end hooks and longitudinal channel stiffeners, they provide secure, durable working platforms in harsh industrial and building environments.

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

High Anti-Slip Traction

Upward-embossed perforated dimples provide maximum boot grip even when wet or oily.

100% Non-Combustible

Essential for petrochemical refineries, hot-work zones, and high-heat sites.

Wind-Lock Security Hooks

Heavy-gauge end hooks with anti-lift safety latches lock securely onto ledgers.

Box-Channel Stiffeners

Longitudinal cold-formed box stiffeners minimize deck deflection under heavy loads.

Ergonomic Aluminum Stairs

Lightweight aluminum stair units with integrated handrails provide safe multi-story access.

Hot-Dip Galvanized Coating

Total zinc coating prevents corrosion and rust across decades of heavy site use.
