Tunnel Engineering

System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Manar Tunnel Formwork Carriage (Tunnel Shutter Traveler) is a fully mechanized, self-propelled hydraulic traveler engineered for casting underground tunnel linings, railway tubes, metro stations, cut-and-cover vehicular tunnels, and water diversion conduits.

Combining a rigid structural steel portal frame with hydraulic folding sidewall panels and crown formwork, the carriage advances autonomously along floor rails in fast, continuous 12-meter casting cycles.

The open portal design allows site dump trucks and service vehicles to drive straight through the carriage while concrete curing and formwork preparation are underway.

Standard Casting Block 6.0 m to 15.0 m Blocks
Operation Mode Full Hydraulic Expansion & Drive
Tunnel Profiles Horseshoe / Circular / Box
Standard Compliance DIN 18218 / EN 12812 / ITA

TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES

KEY ENGINEERING FEATURES

Designed and manufactured for high performance, load safety, and maximum site productivity.

Fully Hydraulic Articulation

Hydraulic rams control sidewall contraction, crown lowering, and vertical leveling.

Self-Propelled Electric Drive

Motorized rail wheels drive the multi-ton carriage smoothly forward between pours.

Clear Portal Drive-Through

High portal clearance leaves open passage for construction vehicles inside the tunnel.

Integrated Concrete Ports

Strategically placed pumping gates and inspection windows ensure void-free crown filling.

100 kN/m² Lateral Pressure

Reinforced steel skin withstands heavy hydrostatic concrete pressures without deflection.

Internal Working Decks

Multi-tier illuminated walkways provide safe inspection, vibrator operation, and maintenance.
