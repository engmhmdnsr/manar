About Manar Al Omran

What started as a scaffolding rental business in Dammam in 1974 is now a formwork and scaffolding company operating across Saudi Arabia, the GCC, and Africa.

Company overview

What MAO does

Manar Al Omran Trading and Industrial Co. Ltd. (MAO) was founded in 1974 as Salem Bin Hindi Al Harbi Establishment. The company rents and sells scaffolding and formwork, provides structural concrete casting services, and offers engineering support for temporary works.

The rental fleet stands at 150,000+ tons of inspected material, held in stockyards across Dammam, Riyadh, Jeddah, Dubai, Bahrain, Cairo, and Johannesburg. MAO also runs in-house engineering for temporary works, 3D BIM planning, and on-site supervision teams.

The company works under ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001, with approvals from Saudi Aramco and SABIC.

Company history

How it started and where it is now

1974

Founded in Dammam

Salem Bin Hindi Al Harbi Establishment opens in the Eastern Province, renting out scaffolding and shuttering to contractors in the Kingdom.

1995

Manufacturing and GCC growth

The company becomes Manar Al Omran Trading and Industrial Co. Ltd. Steel fabrication plants open, and branches appear in Riyadh, Jeddah, UAE, and Bahrain.

2010

Africa and larger fleets

MAO expands into Egypt, South Africa, and Kenya. Rental depots and temporary works engineering divisions grow to match the larger project load.

2024

MAO Industrial Complex

SR 1.2 billion investment in a dedicated scaffolding manufacturing complex in Saudi Arabia, producing systems locally for domestic use and export.

Today

Vision 2030 projects

MAO supplies temporary works to gigaprojects across the Kingdom: Diriyah Square, The Avenues, Haramain Railway, and others. The company continues to invest in 3D BIM, hydraulic climbing systems, and self-climbing technology.
