Project Overview

Highway Flyover, Underpass & Interchange Infrastructure

The Old Makkah Road Interchange is a vital highway transit artery facilitating heavy regional transit between Jeddah and the holy city of Makkah, catering to millions of pilgrim and commercial vehicles annually.

Constructed by Sinobik China, the project featured complex multi-span bridge viaducts and heavy reinforced concrete underpass box structures. Manar Al Omran supplied high-capacity rolling Bridge Table systems and Shorbrace falsework engineered for rapid span repositioning without crane dependency.
