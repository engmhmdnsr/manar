Early Striking Drop-Head System

Drop-head slab formwork system enabling panel stripping after just 3–4 days without disturbing primary props.

Slab Formwork

System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Manar Early Striking Drop-Head System is a high-efficiency modular slab formwork technology designed to drastically reduce inventory requirements and speed up building cycles.

By incorporating an engineered drop-head mechanism atop telescopic props, the main primary beams, secondary beams, and slab panels can be stripped after just 3 to 4 days while the props remain completely undisturbed under the green concrete slab for permanent back-propping.

This cuts on-site formwork material inventory by up to 50% while eliminating micro-cracking and deflection risks on cured slabs.

Striking Time Post-Pour 3 to 4 Days (at 65% strength)
Material Inventory Savings Up to 50% Reduction
Prop Disturbance Zero Re-Propping Disturbance
Standard Compliance DIN EN 12812 / ACI 347R
