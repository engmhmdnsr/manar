Project Overview

Heavy Industrial Manufacturing & Foundation Infrastructure

The Zolen China Industrial Complex in Jizan required massive reinforced concrete foundation blocks, heavy machinery pedestals, and thick blast-resistant retaining walls capable of supporting heavy industrial machinery.

Manar Al Omran supplied heavy Big Steel Panel formwork and robust supporting shoring designed to handle continuous mass concrete pours under harsh coastal ambient conditions for international contractor Zolen China Corporation.

SCOPE OF WORK & SERVICES PROVIDED

How Manar Al Omran delivered heavy steel formwork engineering for demanding industrial foundation blocks.

Mass Concrete Formwork Calculations

Engineered structural calculations and tie-spacing layouts for thick monolithic industrial foundation walls and equipment plinths.

Supplied high-strength Big Steel Panels, heavy steel waler supports, and heavy-duty push-pull alignment props resistant to marine humidity.
