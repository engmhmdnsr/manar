System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Manar Slab Table System is a pre-assembled, crane-handled large-area slab formwork module engineered to achieve high-speed 3-to-4 day floor cycles on multi-story commercial and residential buildings.

Combining high-load telescopic props or shoring frames with H20 timber secondary beams and steel primary girders, complete 30–50 m² table modules are shifted in a single crane pick without dismantling, drastically slashing jobsite labor costs.

Equipped with integrated tilting perimeter handrails, the system delivers high worker safety and architectural fair-faced concrete soffits across dozens of floor cycles.

Standard Table Area 15 m² to 50 m² per Unit
Floor Pour Cycle 3 to 4 Days per Typical Floor
Prop Support Options EN 1065 Props / Shorbrace
Standard Compliance EN 12812 / DIN 18216
