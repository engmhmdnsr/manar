System Overview & Engineering Concept

The Mega Shoring Tower System is Manar Al Omran's ultra-heavy industrial falsework solution engineered for extraordinary concentrated point loads in mega infrastructure projects.

Delivering an astronomical load capacity of up to 1000 kN (100 Tons) per leg (4000 kN per 4-leg tower), Mega Shoring Towers are deployed under segmental precast bridge erections, heavy steel roof truss installations, petrochemical heavy-vessel temporary propping, and hydraulic heavy lifting operations.

Constructed from high-tensile seamless steel pipe sections with heavy bolted connection flanges, the system can be erected free-standing to heights exceeding 40 meters.

Leg Load Capacity Up to 1000 kN per Leg
Tower Load (4-Leg) Up to 4000 kN (400 Tons)
Max Free-Standing Height Exceeding 40.0 Meters
Standard Compliance BS EN 12812 Class C / DIN 18216

High-strength flanged tubular steel sections bolted with Grade 10.9 fasteners.

Integrated Hydraulic Jacking

Allows controlled hydraulic pre-loading, lifting, and synchronous lowering under load.
